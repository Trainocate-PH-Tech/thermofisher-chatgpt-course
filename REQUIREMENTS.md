# Course Requirements

1. Participants’ background in prompting and ChatGPT- Primarily beginner (70%) and intermediate (30%)
2. Expectations-
Equip managers with a practical approach to using ChatGPT as a productivity partner in creating reports and presentation materials.
 
Rather than treating AI-generated content as a finished product, participants should learn to apply a Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) approach: using ChatGPT to accelerate analysis, drafting, structuring, and presentation while maintaining human judgment, verification, and accountability throughout the process.
 
The seminar should focus on three standards for AI-assisted reporting:
Accurate — Information, calculations, conclusions, and claims are verified.
Easy to Understand — Reports communicate the most important message clearly and visually to stakeholders.
Free from Errors — Content is reviewed for factual, logical, numerical, grammatical, and presentation errors before it is shared.
3. Subscription type- Enterprise

**Duration**: 3 Hrs
**Main Tool**: ChatGPT Enterprise
